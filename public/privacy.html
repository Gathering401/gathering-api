Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gathering ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Gathering mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, email address, first and last name, birthdate, phone number, and zip code
  • App content: the groups you join or create, the events you create or RSVP to, and your notification preferences
  • Device information: a push notification token used to deliver notifications to your device

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to operate the App, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Displaying groups and events you are part of
  • Sending you push notifications and emails related to your account and activity (such as event invitations and password resets)
  • Verifying your zip code during signup

We Do Not Sell or Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the App. These services process limited data on our behalf, strictly to provide their function:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: used to deliver push notifications to your device
  • Resend: used to send transactional emails, such as password reset emails
  • Google Places: used to provide location search and autocomplete when creating events
  • Google Cloud Platform: our backend infrastructure and database are hosted on Google Cloud

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data that passes through their systems.

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored in a secured database hosted on Google Cloud. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You may update or delete your account information at any time through the App, or by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at gathering.business.app@gmail.com.
